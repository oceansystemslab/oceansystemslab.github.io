Ocean Systems Lab

Research

Our work spans foundational methods and experiments on real marine robots, in the tank and at sea.

Learning-based control

Reinforcement learning and model predictive control for underwater vehicles that adapt to changing dynamics, payloads and disturbances, from simulation to in-water trials.

Representative papers

  • From Simulation to Reality: Deep Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Docking (2025)
  • Comparative Evaluation of Reinforcement Learning and Model Predictive Control for 6DoF Position Control of an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle (2025)
  • Enhancing AUV Autonomy With Model Predictive Path Integral Control (2023)
  • Adaptive low-level control of autonomous underwater vehicles using deep reinforcement learning (2018)

Underwater perception & SLAM

Sonar and camera processing for 3D reconstruction, mapping and localisation in GPS-denied, low-visibility environments.

Representative papers

  • 3DSSDF: Underwater 3D Sonar Reconstruction Using Signed Distance Functions (2025)
  • FRAGG-Map: Frustum Accelerated GPU-Based Grid Map (2024)
  • Towards Autonomous Subsea Longitudinal Object Detection and Tracking Using a Multi-beam Echo-Sounder (2025)
  • Underwater Visual Acoustic SLAM with Extrinsic Calibration (2021)

Manipulation & intervention

Motion planning and learning for underwater vehicle-manipulator systems carrying out inspection and intervention tasks.

Representative papers

  • Context-Aware Behavior Learning with Heuristic Motion Memory for Underwater Manipulation (2025)
  • Learning-Based Underwater Autonomous Grasping via 3D Point Cloud (2021)
  • Path Planning for Manipulation Using Experience-Driven Random Trees (2021)

Multi-robot & cognitive autonomy

Coordination, planning and shared autonomy for teams of marine robots, including language models and knowledge graphs for mission-level reasoning.

Representative papers

  • Distributed AI Agents for Cognitive Underwater Robot Autonomy (2025)
  • Advancing Shared and Multi-Agent Autonomy in Underwater Missions: Integrating Knowledge Graphs and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (2025)
  • Tethered Multi-Robot Systems in Marine Environments (2025)
  • Planning, Execution, and Adaptation for Multi-Robot Systems using Probabilistic and Temporal Planning (2022)

Simulation & digital twins

Open simulators and digital twins for training and testing marine robots before they get wet.

Representative papers

  • MarineGym: Accelerated Training for Underwater Vehicles with High-Fidelity RL Simulation (2024)
  • Stonefish: Supporting machine learning research in marine robotics (2025)
  • URoBench: Comparative Analyses of Underwater Robotics Simulators from Reinforcement Learning Perspective (2024)
  • Digital Twins Below the Surface: Enhancing Underwater Teleoperation (2024)
